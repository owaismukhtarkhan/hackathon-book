# Human-Robot Interaction

## Overview

Humanoid robots are designed to interact with humans in shared environments, requiring sophisticated interaction capabilities that go beyond simple command execution. This module covers the principles of effective human-robot interaction for humanoid systems.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:
- Design intuitive interaction modalities for humanoid robots
- Implement social behavior and norms in robot actions
- Create natural communication interfaces
- Handle multi-modal interaction scenarios
- Ensure safety during human-robot interaction

## Social Robotics Principles

### Social Norms and Behavior
- Personal space and proxemics
- Gaze and attention mechanisms
- Social conventions and etiquette
- Cultural considerations in interaction

### Expressive Behaviors
- Facial expressions and gestures
- Postural communication
- Voice and speech characteristics
- Emotional expression in robots

## Interaction Modalities

### Natural Language
- Speech recognition and understanding
- Natural language generation
- Dialogue management systems
- Context-aware conversation

### Gestural Communication
- Intentional gestures for communication
- Co-speech gestures
- Deictic gestures (pointing)
- Iconic and metaphoric gestures

### Multimodal Interaction
- Integration of multiple modalities
- Cross-modal attention and focus
- Fusion of sensory inputs
- Consistent behavior across modalities

## Safety and Trust

### Physical Safety
- Collision avoidance during interaction
- Safe motion planning around humans
- Emergency stop mechanisms
- Force and compliance control

### Psychological Safety
- Predictable behavior patterns
- Transparent intention communication
- Error handling and recovery
- Building trust through consistency

## Practical Exercise

Implement a simple human-robot interaction scenario where the humanoid robot responds appropriately to human gestures and verbal commands while maintaining safe distances and appropriate social behaviors. This exercise will demonstrate the integration of multiple interaction modalities.
